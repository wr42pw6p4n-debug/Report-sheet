⚠ Get this into Safari first

On iPad, "Open in Safari" isn't in the Files long-press menu

1. Files app → find this file → tap once to preview it (Quick Look opens)
2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow, top-right of Quick Look) — not a long-press
3. In the share sheet, scroll the row of app icons and tap Safari
4. Once it's open in Safari, tap Aa in the address bar → Add to Home Screen — it'll then open full-screen like a real app, ready for the crane yard, no re-downloading needed.
